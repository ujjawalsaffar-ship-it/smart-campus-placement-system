Business Rule Triggers (SQLite / JDBC)

Trigger 1: Eligibility Validation

BEFORE INSERT ON applications

Checks student's CGPA ≥ job's min_cgpa. If not, fires RAISE(ABORT) to reject application.

Trigger 2: Bidirectional Placement Synchronization

AFTER UPDATE OF status / AFTER DELETE ON applications

Automatically sets students.placement_status = 'Placed' when an offer is 'Selected'. If that offer is later rejected or deleted, automatically reverts the student's status back to 'Unplaced' (unless they hold another active 'Selected' offer).
