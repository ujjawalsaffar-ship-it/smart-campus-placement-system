Relational Schema (3NF)

students (student_id PK, name, email UNIQUE, department, cgpa, placement_status)
companies (company_id PK, company_name UNIQUE, industry, location, contact_email)
job_postings (job_id PK, company_id FK, role_title, min_cgpa, package_lpa, deadline)
applications (application_id PK, student_id FK, job_id FK, status, application_date, UNIQUE(student_id, job_id))

Business Rule Triggers (SQLite / JDBC)

Trigger 1: Eligibility Validation

BEFORE INSERT ON applications

Checks student's CGPA ≥ job's min_cgpa. If not, fires RAISE(ABORT) to reject application.

Trigger 2: Auto Status Synchronization

AFTER UPDATE OF status ON applications

When status changes to 'Selected', automatically updates students.placement_status = 'Placed'.
